# Functional Documentation

## What Is CarLoan?

CarLoan is a web application that lets you apply for a car loan online and track your application status in real time — no branch visit required.

---

## Features

### Apply for a Car Loan

Submit a new loan application entirely online in three short steps:

1. **Personal Information** — Enter your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. **Financial Information** — Provide your employment status, annual income, and employer name (optional).
3. **Vehicle & Loan Details** — Enter the vehicle make, model, and year, along with the vehicle price, your down payment, the loan amount you need, and your preferred repayment term.

Once submitted, you will receive a unique **Tracking ID** (e.g. `CAR-A3F7B2D9`). Keep this safe — you will need it to check your application status.

---

### Track Your Application

Use your Tracking ID at any time to see the current status of your application.

**Application statuses:**

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Submitted** | Your application has been received and is waiting to be reviewed. |
| **Under Review** | Our team is actively reviewing your application. |
| **Approved** | Your car loan has been approved. |
| **Rejected** | Your application was not approved at this time. |

The tracking page also shows a progress timeline so you can see exactly where your application is in the process, along with a summary of the vehicle and loan details you submitted.

---

## How to Get Started

Open the application in your browser. The home page gives you two options:

- **Apply for a Loan** — Start a new application.
- **Track Your Application** — Check the status of an existing application.

You can also reach these pages at any time using the navigation links at the top of every page.

---

## What You Will Need Before Applying

Before starting your application, it helps to have the following ready:

- Your personal contact details (email, phone, date of birth)
- Your employment status and approximate annual income
- The make, model, year, and price of the vehicle you want to finance
- How much you plan to put down as a down payment
- The loan amount and repayment term you want (terms available: 1 – 7 years)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I apply for more than one loan?**
Yes. Each submission creates a separate application with its own unique Tracking ID.

**What is the minimum loan amount?**
The minimum loan amount is $1,000. The vehicle price must also be at least $1,000.

**How old does the vehicle need to be?**
The application accepts vehicles from model year 1980 up to the current year.

**What happens after I submit?**
Your application is logged immediately with a status of *Submitted*. A team member will review it and update the status to *Approved* or *Rejected*. You can check back at any time using your Tracking ID.

**I lost my Tracking ID. What do I do?**
Unfortunately there is no way to look up a Tracking ID without the original value. Make sure to note it down or copy it when your application is confirmed.
